Decision networks

Each column is a moment where real participants on the same task chose differently. Each line is one conversation, passing through the choice it made and ending at whether the task passed. Boxes with a bold outline are choices a person made that no simulated conversation made.

48 tasks, 3 participants and 8 simulated conversations each (gpt-5.2 agent). Moments and choices were labelled by a model from the transcripts; tasks are ordered by how many outcome-changing choices only people made. Hover a box or a line to trace it.
